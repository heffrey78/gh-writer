[Tomas](#char_t0ma5h) did not look up from the lathe when she came in. He never had. "You'll want to know about the plans," he said.

"I want to know who has them."

He switched the lathe off and the silence came down like a dropped sheet. "The office was locked. The cabinet was locked. Your father's drawings were in it on Sunday and gone on Monday."

"Who has a key?"

He wiped his hands for a long time. "Three people," he said. "I'm one. You're not."
