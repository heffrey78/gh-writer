[Ben](#char_b3n0vs) met her at the toll house with two coffees and a grin that was a size too large for him.

"They're only measuring," he said, before she could ask. "The council wants to know what it's worth. That's all."

"It's worth what it carries." She put her palm flat against the rail. The iron was warm from the day and humming faintly, the way it always had when the wind came up the valley.

"Spoken like Dad." He laughed, and it was nearly the old laugh. Nearly.

At the seventh pier she stopped and looked down at the fresh paint around the bolt heads. Somebody had been checking the rivets. Somebody who knew which ones to check.
