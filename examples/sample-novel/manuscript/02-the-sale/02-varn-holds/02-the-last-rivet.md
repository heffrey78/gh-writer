At dawn [Ben](#char_b3n0vs) was waiting at the toll house with two coffees, and this time he didn't grin.

"Seventh pier," he said. "I know which rivets they checked. I can show you which ones they didn't."

They worked until the sun was well up, Tomas reading the gauge in a voice that shook a little now, and Ada did not ask her brother why. Some questions are only bridges you have to walk across.
