[Mirela Kost](#char_m1re1a) had the plans spread across Tomas's bench as if they were hers, which, Ada supposed, they now were.

"Your father was a fine engineer," Mirela said. "For 1931. I'm offering you the chance to be one for this century."

"By knocking his bridge down."

"By building a better one. With your name on it, if you like." She smiled. "Your brother's debt goes away either way. I'd rather it went away with your help."
