The wind came off the river hard enough to lean on.

[Ada](#char_7f3k2q) saw him before he saw her. He was standing at the seventh pier with his collar up, looking at the water as if it owed him something.

"How much did she give you?" she said.

[Ben](#char_b3n0vs) didn't pretend. That was the worst of it, later: that he didn't even pretend. "Enough to make the ledger go away."

"They were his drawings."

"They were paper, Ada. You can't eat paper. You can't heat a house with it." He turned to face her at last. "You left. I stayed. Staying costs money."

Below them the river went on doing what it had always done, which was to wait.
